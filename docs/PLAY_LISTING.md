## 3. Full description (max 4000 characters)

### English

```
SriEdu brings the books, papers and practice a Sri Lankan student actually
needs into one clean, fast app — in Sinhala, Tamil and English.

STUDY WITH THE OFFICIAL SOURCES
• Every textbook is listed grade by grade (1–13), by subject and by medium.
• Past papers are organised the way exams are: O/L, A/L, Grade 5 Scholarship
  and term tests, with subject, year and medium filters.
• Each paper shows how many students downloaded it, its file size and a direct
  link to its marking scheme.
• Papers and textbooks open at the official publisher — the National Institute
  of Education and the Department of Examinations — so you are always reading
  the paper that was actually set.

QUIZZES THAT SHOW YOU WHAT YOU MISSED
• Timed multiple-choice quizzes by subject and year.
• A result screen with your score, the time you took, and a review of every
  wrong answer with the explanation.
• Try again as often as you like. Progress is stored on your phone, not on
  someone's server — SriEdu has no accounts and no sign-in.

BUILD A HABIT
• A daily Streak that grows when you read or finish a quiz.
• One free "streak freeze" each week for the days life gets in the way.
• Badges for 7-day streaks, quiz mastery and saving ten papers offline.
• A study planner: pick subjects and time slots, get a reminder when it is time.

READ OFFLINE, ON A SLOW CONNECTION
• Save supported books and papers for offline reading — useful when data is
  expensive or the signal is weak.
• Your last page in every book is remembered, so you resume where you stopped.
• My Library shows downloads, favourites, history and how much space the app is
  using, with a one-tap clear.

MADE FOR SRI LANKAN DEVICES
• Sinhala and Tamil fonts are built in, so text is shaped correctly rather than
  showing boxes.
• Works on Android 5.0 and up, and stays small (under 30 MB).
• Light and dark themes, adjustable text size, and a student name for the
  greeting.

ABOUT ADS AND PRIVACY
• Ads are limited to a small banner on list screens. There are no ads at all
  inside the PDF reader or during a quiz.
• No account, no personal data, nothing uploaded about what you study.

SriEdu is an independent study companion. It links to official government
publications and does not re-publish copyrighted textbooks or past papers.
```

### Content rating questionnaire

Answer honestly; the answers below match the implementation:

* Violence, sexuality, profanity, drugs, gambling: **No** to all.
* User-generated content or chat: **No** (there is no messaging and no sharing).
* Personal information sharing: **No**.
* Ads: **Yes**, contained in the app (banner) and full-screen (interstitial after
  downloads).
* Expected rating: **Everyone / 3+**.

### Target audience and Families policy

The app is for school students, which includes children under 13 (grades 1–8).
If you declare an audience that includes children:

* AdMob must be configured for **child-directed treatment** (tag the app in the
  AdMob console, and keep `AppConfig.adsSupportedPlatformsOnly` true).
* No interest-based advertising, and no interstitials on screens a child is
  reading — which is already the design: no ads in the reader or the quiz.
* Consider shipping with ads disabled (`--dart-define=ADS_ENABLED=false`) for the
  first release; the app is fully usable without them and it removes an entire
  policy class from your review.
